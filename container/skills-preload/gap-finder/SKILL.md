# Gap Finder — The Angle Only You Can Take

Recreating what's working builds consistency and confidence. But at some point, the real asset is what only you know.

The gap is the space between what's trending and what only you can say. It shows up three ways: a knowledge gap nobody's applied to your niche yet, an industry gap where your expertise transfers somewhere unexpected, or a perspective gap where a popular take is missing something obvious to you.

This skill finds it.

---

## Phase 1: Load the Brain

Read all of these before doing anything:
- `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`
- `~/.claude/brand-voice.md`
- `~/.claude/icp.md`
- `~/.claude/offer.md`
- `~/.claude/what-works.md`

You need the full picture — what they know, who they serve, what they've been posting, and what's already working.

If CLAUDE.md is missing or sparse:
> "Your content brain doesn't have enough in it yet for me to find your specific gap. Run /brain-builder first — the more I know about your background and expertise, the more precise this gets."

---

## Phase 2: Get the Context

**Option A — Member pastes trending content:**
They paste a post, thread, or video that's getting traction in their niche. This is the anchor — you're finding the gap relative to what's already out there.

**Option B — No content pasted:**
Run a web search for what's performing in their niche right now. Use their CLAUDE.md to identify the right keywords and platforms.

Search pattern:
- Reddit: top posts in their niche subreddits, past week
- LinkedIn: top posts in their niche, past 7 days
- YouTube: trending videos in their niche, past 30 days

Identify the 3–5 most common angles being covered. These are what everybody's saying. The gap is what nobody's saying — but the member can.

---

## Phase 3: Find the Three Gaps

Analyze the trending content against the member's CLAUDE.md context. Look for these specifically:

**The Knowledge Gap**
What expertise does the member have that nobody is applying to the trending topic? What do they know from their specific field, client work, or experience that would reframe how people think about this?

Signs of a knowledge gap: "Everyone's talking about X, but nobody's mentioned Y — and I've been using Y with clients for three years."

**The Industry Gap**
What principle or framework exists in the member's world that would be completely new, surprising, or immediately useful to people in an adjacent space?

Signs of an industry gap: The member works in field A. The trending topic is being covered by people in field B. The member knows something from field A that directly applies but hasn't crossed over yet.

**The Perspective Gap**
What's the popular take missing? What does everyone assume to be true that the member's experience contradicts? What's the "yes, but actually..." reaction they have when they see this content?

Signs of a perspective gap: "That take is only half right. Here's the part they're not saying."
