## The engagement
- **Client:** Isaacs & Isaacs Personal Injury Lawyers ("The Hammer" — Darryl Isaacs)
- **Role:** Agency-side, Google Ads only ("The PPCs," as Darryl called them)
- **Duration:** ~1 year
- **Monthly spend:** ~$150,000/month
- **Total managed:** ~$1.8M
- **Account size context:** Isaacs & Isaacs is a 100+ employee firm with 11 offices across KY/IN/OH, $2B+ in lifetime settlements, $15M annual revenue, famous for Super Bowl ads and "The Hammer" persona

## The performance story
- **Held CAC to 30% or less of their YoY benchmark for 70% of the year**
- Didn't have CRM access — used a collaborative attribution model
- Client sent signed cases weekly; Steve back-calculated CPA against ad spend
- This is full-funnel attribution with imperfect data — exactly the kind of intake-tracking problem RR Digital obsesses over

## How it ended
- Isaacs consolidated all marketing under one agency that handled everything (TV, radio, billboards, programmatic, PPC, the works)
- Steve was let go as part of agency consolidation, NOT performance
- Performance was solid the entire engagement

## Talking points for RR Digital interview
- Speaks fluent PI marketing — understands cost-per-signed-case, intake collaboration, CRM-less attribution
- Has worked on the exact problem RR Digital's whole pitch is built around (case economics, not vanity metrics)
- Familiar with the celebrity-attorney media-buy archetype
- Knows the politics of PI firms — they consolidate when they want one throat to choke
