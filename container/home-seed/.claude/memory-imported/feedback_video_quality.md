Blotato's AI Story Video template produces functional but recognizably AI content (stock AI images + generic TTS voice).

**Why:** Coworker feedback that the video quality is "not very good." AI-generated visuals + generic voice won't build a premium brand long-term.

**How to apply:**
- NO voice cloning — Steve declined this approach
- LinkedIn should use static graphics instead of video (Steve confirmed this preference)
- ALWAYS spell out words phonetically in video scripts to prevent AI mispronunciation (e.g. "a16z" → "A sixteen Z", "TSMC" → "T S M C", "ICLR" → "I C L R", "$10B" → "ten billion dollars", "Pichai" → "Pih-chai", "ASML" → "A S M L")
- Long-term goal: face-to-cam delivery for maximum engagement
- Consider CapCut/Canva for post-processing Blotato output (logo watermark, transitions, trending audio)
