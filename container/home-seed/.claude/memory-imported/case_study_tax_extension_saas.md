## The engagement
- **Client:** Tax extension SaaS (DO NOT name the client publicly — Steve confirmed it cannot be mentioned by name)
- **What to call it externally:** "A tax extension SaaS" or "an IRS extension filing platform"
- **Cadence:** 3 years running, seasonal peak around tax deadlines
- **Spend pattern (per year):**
  - 1 month at $1,000,000/month (peak — tax deadline crunch)
  - 2 months at $200,000/month (shoulder)
  - $1.4M per year × 3 years = $4.2M total managed
- **Flagship metric:** **$256,000 in profit in 48 hours** (single campaign window during peak)

## Why this is the hero story
- The 48-hour profit number is concrete, memorable, and rare in marketing storytelling
- Demonstrates ability to scale spend 5x in a narrow window without losing efficiency
- Demonstrates seasonal-peak campaign architecture — building a machine that survives 5x volume swings
- "Profit," not "revenue" or "ROAS" — Steve was measuring to the bottom line

- Use when interviewer wants to test attribution rigor (you measured to profit, not just clicks)
