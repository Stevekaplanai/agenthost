### Who I Am at a Human Level
*A document for Claude — not my offer, not my audience. Me.*

---

## 1. How did you end up doing what you do?

I started in digital marketing because I could see the machine — the underlying logic of why an ad works, why a funnel leaks, why a bidder makes a bad decision. I spent nine years running Click Then Convert, managing $50M+ in spend across 100+ clients, and getting very good at a thing most people get wrong: attribution. When AI hit a real inflection point, I didn't pivot — I accelerated. I stopped managing campaigns for other people and started building the tools I always wished existed. Now I run GTMVP, Attribyte, and a portfolio of 9+ live AI products I built solo — the same operator instincts, just with a 10x build speed I never had before.

---

## 2. What did you walk away from to get here — and what was the moment?

I walked away from the agency. Nine years. A real business with real revenue and a team I'd built from nothing. The moment was somewhere in 2024 — I was sitting inside a 100-person marketing org at Market Pro, launching three AI regtech platforms simultaneously (Facia.ai, TheKYB.com, AMLWatcher.com), and I realized I was doing the highest-leverage work of my career inside someone else's org chart. The AI build cycle had collapsed from six months to two weeks. I had the skills, the reps, the hard-won pattern recognition — and for the first time, the tools to act alone. The agency model made sense before AI. After AI it felt like leaving money on the table every single day I wasn't building for myself.

---

## 3. What's the opinion you hold that most people in your industry would push back on?

**Last-click attribution isn't just wrong — it's a tax on growth that most companies are voluntarily paying and calling it "data-driven."**

The industry loves Google's reporting because Google's reporting makes Google look good. Ninety percent of the clients I've worked with had Brand Search credited with 80–100% of their revenue — and were cutting the channels that actually warmed the buyer. LinkedIn, YouTube, top-of-funnel display — written off as "awareness spend" because the last click happened somewhere else. I built Attribyte because I was tired of watching smart founders make budget decisions based on a lie that the platform selling them the ads was happy to keep telling. The real controversial take: Smart Bidding works great — but only if you stop feeding it garbage. Most accounts do not have a spend problem. They have a signal problem. Fix the signal, and the machine does the rest.

---

## 4. Who do you most want to help, and why that specific person?

The post-agency or post-corporate operator who's gone independent and is running serious paid media — $20K to $300K/month — but still making decisions like they're inside an agency with a team around them. They know enough to be dangerous. They've got real results on their resume. But they're flying blind on attribution, they've never audited their bidding signals from first principles, and they're too close to their own accounts to see what's broken. That person wastes more money per month than most people spend in a year — and they don't know it yet. That's exactly who I was for longer than I'd like to admit.

---

## 5. What are you still figuring out?

How to scale two products at the same time without one cannibalizing the other's attention. GTMVP and Attribyte solve different problems for the same buyer — which sounds like a bundle but feels like a distraction when I'm the one building, shipping, and selling. I'm good at execution. I'm less certain about sequencing. Which one gets the brand? Which one gets the cold outreach? Which one earns the case study? I know how to build. I'm still figuring out which thing to build *for*, when both things are real.

---

## 6. What would you tell someone who's where you were 3 years ago?

The agency isn't the ceiling — you just can't see past it from inside. The operator skills you've built running other people's accounts are worth ten times what you think, but only if you package them as a system, not a service. Stop trading hours. Build the thing that works when you're asleep. And don't wait until you feel ready to go AI-first — you will never feel ready, and the gap between you and the person who started six months ago is already closing faster than you think. The window is open. The tools are real. The only thing standing between where you are and where you want to be is the decision to stop managing and start building.

---

## 7. "I built this because I was sick of ___."

I built this because I was sick of watching platforms grade their own homework.

Sick of Google telling you your Performance Max campaign is performing — right up until you look at where the budget actually went. Sick of last-click attribution surviving 2024 as if the buyer journey is still a straight line. Sick of agencies charging retainers to maintain accounts they never audited from the ground up. Sick of founders making bet-the-company budget decisions based on data that was designed to keep them spending, not to help them grow.

I built GTMVP and Attribyte because the tools that should have existed for operators like me — didn't. So I built them myself. That's it.
