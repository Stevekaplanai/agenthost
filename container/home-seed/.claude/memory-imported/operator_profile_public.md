# Steve Kaplan AI

## Operator Profile + Brand Brief

---

## Who He Is

Steve Kaplan is a paid media operator and AI builder who has spent 14 years in paid media (since 2014 at PPC Professionals) and 14 years overall in digital marketing (since 2012). He managed over $50M in lifetime ad spend across Google, Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn. He averaged a 3.2x ROAS across more than 100 clients and ran accounts at $2M+ per month with a sustained 10:1 ROAS at scale. He is Google Partner certified, Facebook Blueprint certified, Klaviyo Partner certified, and an Amazon bestselling author.

He ran his own performance marketing agency, Click Then Convert, for nearly 10 years (2015-2024) before closing it to go full-time on AI products. The agency operated across B2B, ecommerce, direct-to-consumer, professional services, and regulated verticals including financial services, personal injury, addiction treatment, rideshare, and regtech. The track record includes CAC reductions of up to 80% across multiple verticals.

In 2024 and 2025, Steve shifted from operator to builder. He shipped 9 AI products solo using Claude Code as his engineering partner. He is the founder of GTMVP, the paid media OS for B2B founders still managing their own ad accounts. He built Attribyte, a multi-touch attribution, marketing mix modeling, and incrementality platform for mid-market to enterprise B2B SaaS. He also built ClaudeSkillsHQ, VentureVault, SynapMarketing, CompetitorIntelligence.co, and several others.

He is based in the Miami-Fort Lauderdale area and works full-time on his own products.

## How He Works

Steve builds and operates using Claude Code as his primary engineering partner. He works with conventional commits, ships to production on short cycles, and iterates on real usage data rather than roadmap assumptions. He does not build prototypes. Everything he ships is a live product with real users.

Primary stack: React, TypeScript, Supabase, Python, Claude Code.

He does not use dev teams or agency structures for his own products. The constraint is also the edge: he builds exactly what an operator needs because he is the operator.

## Operating Principles

**Grand Slam Offers.** Every campaign starts with an offer so clear that declining feels like leaving money on the table. Clever ad copy is downstream of a compelling offer.

**Leads First.** Lead volume changes what is possible in a business. Systems that generate qualified leads on demand are the foundation of the work, not a feature.

**Builder Not Advisor.** Most consultants ship a deck. Steve ships the system. He writes the code, deploys the infrastructure, and measures the output.

**3.2x Average ROAS.** The lifetime number across all clients. Not a cherry-picked peak quarter. The standard of what good looks like.
