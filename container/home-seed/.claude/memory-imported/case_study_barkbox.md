## The engagement
- **Client:** BarkBox (barkbox.com — D2C subscription, dog products)
- **Years:** 2019-2020
- **Channel:** Google Ads only
- **Monthly spend:** ~$400,000/month average
- **Total managed:** Approximately $9-10M over the engagement
- **Started:** With one side of the business; won ALL of BarkBox's Google Ads business through performance
- **How it ended:** BarkBox brought the entire marketing team in-house (org maturity, not performance)

## Why this matters in interviews
- D2C subscription = brutal LTV/CAC math, no room to waste
- $400K/mo is genuine enterprise-scale paid search
- "Won all the Google Ads business" = expansion within an account by performance, not relationship politics
- Demonstrates ability to operate at high spend without losing efficiency
- BarkBox is a recognizable consumer brand — name-drop has weight
