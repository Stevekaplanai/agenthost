Steve, 2026-07-27 (launch morning, after clocking two em dashes in a posted Reddit
reply): "Dead giveaway you wrote. Instantly — humans don't use those at all. You
can use an en dash though, I abuse the heck out of those."

Extends the original 2026-06 rule (which was scoped to ElevenLabs voice scripts).

**How to apply:**
- NO em dashes (—) in anything that ships in Steve's name to a public surface:
  Reddit posts/replies, HN, PH comments, directory listings, tweets, LinkedIn,
  emails, site copy. Use periods, commas, colons, parentheses, or EN dashes (–).
- En dashes (–) are explicitly blessed — they read as Steve.
- Internal docs, code comments, artifacts for Steve's own eyes: em dashes fine.
- Sweep any draft before delivering it as paste-ready public copy.

**Why:** em dashes are a statistically loud AI tell in 2026; one em dash can
undermine an authentic builder post more than any wording choice.
